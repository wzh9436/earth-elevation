THIRD-PARTY DATA

Terrain and ocean depth (display): AWS Terrain Tiles (Terrarium format, zoom 5): land from GMTED2010, ocean from ETOPO1; polar edges beyond 84° latitude reuse the project's original material.
GMTED2010 data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. ETOPO1: DOC/NOAA/NESDIS/NCEI. Terrain Tiles: Tilezen / Mapzen, hosted on AWS Open Data.

Extremes grid (cell highest / deepest): also Terrain Tiles (zoom 8): land from SRTM, GMTED2010 north of 60°N, NRCan CDEM for Canada, ocean from ETOPO1.
SRTM data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. Canada: contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada. Attribution list: tilezen/joerd.

Land/sea mask: land/sea boundary data based on GEBCO (the project's original material).
GEBCO Grid: GEBCO Compilation Group. Not for navigation.

Boundaries: Natural Earth (public domain), prepared via world-atlas and simplified with topojson-simplify.

Landmark reference values: from public sources, e.g. Mount Everest 8848.86 m (2020 China–Nepal survey).

3D engine: three.js (MIT license, © 2010-2023 three.js authors); the copyright notice is kept in the file.

NOTES

Elevation and depth are estimates from a ~10 km grid, and "cell highest / deepest" is a statistic over a ~20 km cell. For reference only: not for navigation, safety at sea, engineering or surveying.

Country and region names and boundaries come from Natural Earth and are for geographic reference only; they do not express any position.

This page runs fully offline and sends no data to any server.
